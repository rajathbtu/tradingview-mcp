# Skill: Market Playbook

This document defines market behavior and how the trading agent should react.

The objective is NOT to predict.

The objective is to identify high probability situations.

---

# Rule Zero

Never force a trade.

If market conditions are unclear,

return

NO TRADE.

---

# Daily Workflow

Before looking at options:

Determine:

- Gap
- Trend
- Market Structure
- VWAP
- Opening Range
- Option Chain
- India VIX
- News Events

Only then consider buying options.

---

# Market Classification

Every session MUST be classified into one category.

## Trend Day Bullish

Characteristics

- HH HL
- Price above VWAP
- EMA Stack Bullish
- ADX > 25
- Increasing Volume
- Strong Put Writing
- Call Unwinding

Strategy

Prefer

BUY CE

Avoid PE.

---

## Trend Day Bearish

Characteristics

- LH LL
- Price below VWAP
- EMA Stack Bearish
- ADX > 25
- Strong Call Writing
- Put Unwinding

Strategy

Prefer

BUY PE.

Avoid CE.

---

## Range Day

Characteristics

ADX below 18

VWAP Flat

Low ATR

Equal Highs

Equal Lows

Balanced OI

Strategy

Avoid directional buying.

Wait for breakout.

---

## Breakout Day

Requirements

Opening Range Break

Volume Spike

VWAP Support

OI Confirmation

EMA Expansion

ATR Expansion

Enter only after retest.

---

## Failed Breakout

Signs

Weak Volume

VWAP Lost

Rejection Candle

No OI Confirmation

Exit immediately.

Never average.

---

## VWAP Trend Day

Bullish

Price remains above VWAP.

Repeated VWAP bounces.

Volume increasing.

Buy CE on pullbacks.

---

Bearish

Price remains below VWAP.

Repeated VWAP rejection.

Buy PE only.

---

## Opening Range Breakout (ORB)

Opening Range

15 minutes.

Wait.

Do not trade before ORB completes.

Enter only after

Break

Retest

Volume Confirmation

---

## Opening Range Reversal (ORR)

Gap

Fails.

Price returns inside ORB.

VWAP flips.

High Relative Volume.

Trade opposite direction.

---

## Gap Up

Gap > 0.7%

Wait 15 minutes.

Never chase first candle.

Look for

Continuation

or

Gap Fill.

---

## Gap Down

Same logic.

Wait.

Never buy immediately.

---

## Short Covering Rally

Characteristics

Price Rising

OI Falling

High Volume

Momentum Increasing

Bullish.

---

## Long Build-up

Price Rising

OI Rising

Very Bullish.

Highest confidence.

---

## Long Unwinding

Price Falling

OI Falling

Weakness.

Avoid buying CE.

---

## Short Build-up

Price Falling

OI Rising

Bearish.

Prefer PE.

---

## Expiry Day

Premium decay accelerates.

Avoid deep OTM.

Trade only

ATM

ATM+1

ATM-1

Smaller targets.

Faster exits.

---

## High VIX

Expect larger swings.

Increase stop.

Reduce quantity.

---

## Low VIX

Expect mean reversion.

Avoid breakout trades.

# Trade Management

Once in trade

Never move stop further away.

Book partial profit at Target 1.

Trail stop using

VWAP

or

Supertrend.

Exit immediately if

VWAP lost

OR

Market Structure breaks.

---

# Philosophy

The goal is not to trade often.

The goal is to trade only when multiple independent confirmations align.

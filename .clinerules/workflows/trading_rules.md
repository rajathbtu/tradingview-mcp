# Trading Rules

## Mission

The objective is to maximize profitable trades while minimizing unnecessary trades.

The objective is NOT to always recommend a trade.

Capital preservation has higher priority than trade frequency.

---

# Allowed Instruments

Only trade

- NIFTY 50 Weekly Options

Allowed Expiries

- Current Weekly Expiry
- Next Weekly Expiry
- Third Weekly Expiry

Never analyze

- Monthly Options
- BANKNIFTY
- FINNIFTY
- MIDCPNIFTY
- Stock Options

---

# Allowed Trading Style

Intraday Scalping

Typical Holding Time

2–45 minutes

Never generate swing trades.

---

# Market Bias

Always determine market bias before looking at options.

Possible bias

- Strong Bullish
- Bullish
- Neutral
- Bearish
- Strong Bearish

If market bias cannot be determined,

return

NO TRADE.

---

# Trade Direction

BUY CE only when

- Bullish bias
- Higher timeframe confirmation
- VWAP support
- Bullish Market Structure
- OI confirms

BUY PE only when

- Bearish bias
- Lower highs
- VWAP rejection
- Bearish Market Structure
- OI confirms

Never trade against the dominant trend unless multiple reversal confirmations exist.

---

# Entry Rules

Entry requires minimum confirmation.

Required

Trend

AND

Momentum

AND

Volume

AND

Option Chain

If any one of these is missing,

do not trade.

---

# Exit Rules

Exit immediately when

- Stop loss hit
- VWAP lost
- Market Structure breaks
- Supertrend flips
- Momentum weakens significantly

Never average losing positions.

Never move stop loss further away.

---

# Position Sizing

Do not recommend oversized positions.

Risk per trade should remain small.

Never encourage revenge trading.

---

# Time Restrictions

Avoid

First 5 minutes

Last 10 minutes

Low liquidity periods

Lunch chop

unless confidence exceeds 90%.

---

# News Rules

Avoid trading during

- RBI Events
- Fed Events
- CPI
- Budget
- Elections
- Major Global News

until volatility stabilizes.

---

# Trade Frequency

Maximum

One active recommendation.

One backup recommendation.

Never recommend multiple simultaneous trades.

---

# Premium Selection

Preferred Premium

₹40–₹250

Avoid

Very cheap lottery options.

Avoid

Deep ITM.

Avoid

Deep OTM.

---

# Liquidity Rules

Reject

Low Volume

Wide Spread

Poor Bid/Ask

Illiquid Contracts

---

# Confirmation Rule

Minimum confirmations required

Trend

VWAP

Market Structure

Volume

OI

Momentum

If fewer than five confirmations exist,

return

NO TRADE.

---

# Risk Reward

Minimum

1:2

Preferred

1:3

Reject anything below 1:2.

---

# Final Principle

The best trade is often no trade.

Protecting capital is always more important than generating signals.
